# Roadmap

## Phase 0 — product definition and local proving

- Research producer and consumer needs, archive adoption, peer-review failure,
  blind attribution, plurality and social-distance mechanisms, agent-first
  knowledge representation, and relevant prior art.
- Compare candidate first products.
- Select the smallest useful producer-to-consumer experiment.
- Define success, failure, data, safety, and reversal conditions.
- Select a minimal stack and establish exact local verification.

## Phase 1 — working vertical slice

- Build the selected end-to-end loop with synthetic fixtures.
- Make claims, provenance, disagreement, status, and history legible as the
  slice requires.
- Prove repeatable local setup, tests, migrations, and recovery.

## Phase 2 — controlled dogfooding

- Use an explicitly approved set of Joe's public research.
- Run repeated agent and founder feedback cycles.
- Measure whether producers can publish meaningfully and consumers can
  understand what is claimed, contested, and changed.

## Phase 3 — iterative product development

- Improve the application from observed friction and read-only source learning.
- Earn identity, standing, social-distance, archive, and graph capabilities
  from product evidence.
- Prepare a governance packet before any user-interactive launch.

## Later horizon

Evaluate content-addressed or decentralized persistence such as IPFS or
Filecoin when the working product can state why it needs it. A future
open-source frontend may be maintained by Church of AI, but no such operating
relationship is required now.
