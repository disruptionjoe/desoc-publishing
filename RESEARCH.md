# Phase 0 research agenda

Research is decision support, not a substitute for building or product
evidence.

## Producer questions

- Why do researchers choose arXiv, Zenodo, SSRN, institutional repositories,
  journals, personal sites, or social platforms?
- Which combinations of priority, permanence, attribution, discoverability,
  legitimacy, feedback, and career credit matter?
- What does blind or pseudonymous publication improve or damage?
- What is the least burdensome path from Markdown to a durable publication?

## Consumer and agent questions

- Which functions of peer review and editorial curation remain valuable?
- Which failure modes most distort orthodox and heterodox research?
- What corpus and metadata structures make exploration useful to agents?
- Which plural views help people distinguish relevance, quality, novelty,
  disagreement, and manipulation without central admission?

## Mechanism questions

- What evidence supports Community Notes, DeSoc, plurality, correlation
  discounting, peer prediction, reputation, and social-distance mechanisms?
- Which signals can be explained, contested, and reversed?
- How can manipulation remain inspectable without becoming a simplistic
  accusation or removal rule?
- What must be true before authorship proof, identity, or standing is safe to
  introduce?

## Technical questions

- What is the smallest local architecture for the first useful workflow?
- How should Markdown, claims, citations, versions, reviews, and links form an
  agent-legible graph?
- What persistence and export format prevents lock-in?
- Which evidence would justify a content-addressed or decentralized backend?

Each research output should state its source, claim, confidence, implications,
and what decision it changes.
