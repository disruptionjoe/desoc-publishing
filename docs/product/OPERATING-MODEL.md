# DeSoc Product Operating Model

The governed object is a product hypothesis or slice, not a lifecycle stage.

```text
problem shape -> solution design -> implement -> human-use validate ->
keep, revise, revert, or reshape
```

Research, build, and evaluation are selectable treatments inside Lane 1.
Different evidence may move the object backward or forward without creating a
new Lane. Active plans, product specifications, evaluations, `STATUS.md`, and
`LANE-STATE.yaml` remain authoritative.

Implementation evidence proves mechanics. Product validation asks whether a
producer or consumer can achieve the intended behavior. Readiness, authorized
deployment, real use, and external outcome remain distinct. Every result
returns to the current product hypothesis and stays owned until disposition.

Lane A reprioritizes the product portfolio, checks contract and evidence fit,
protects constitutional and external-action boundaries, and escalates
ownerless returns.
